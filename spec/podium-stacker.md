Revised Layout Specifications: Podium Stack
Arrangement: Horizontal "Podium" layout.

Center (#1): Primary focus. Largest scale (e.g., 110%–120% size). Highest z-index to sit on top.

Left (#2): Slightly smaller. Positioned behind the center item with a partial overlap (negative horizontal margin/offset).

Right (#3): Same size as #2. Positioned behind the center item with a partial overlap.

Visual Hierarchy:

Layering: Center item overlaps the inner edges of the side items.

Alignment: All items bottom-aligned or center-aligned vertically.

Component Structure: Each "slot" consists of a Title/Label paired with its corresponding Image Poster.

Animation Sequence:

Logic: Animate pairs (Label + Poster) as a single unit.

Stagger: 300ms delay between each unit.

Order: Center (#1) first, followed by Left (#2), then Right (#3).
